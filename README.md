# Portfolio

## Vibe Check
Vibe Check is a location-based social app designed for everyone, with a focus on LGBTQIA+ inclusion and a 1960s-inspired ethos of love, connection, and openness. It supports people who want to date, hook up, or simply meet their person, and it encourages respectful conversation with anyone on the platform.

### Core principles
- Inclusive by design: welcoming straight, LGBTQIA+, poly, pansexual, sapiosexual, and other identities.
- Open connection: chat with anyone without distance-based subscription tiers.
- Positive culture: promotes kindness, consent, and safe interactions.
- Alpha build: more features are on the way as the app evolves.

### Key features
- Location-based discovery similar to grid-style apps.
- One-time purchase model: $5.99 lifetime access.
- No in-app purchases or tiered distance unlocks.
- Friendly, chat-first experience for casual or serious connections.
- 18+ only with date-of-birth age verification.
- iPhone sign-up and distribution via the Apple App Store only.
- Safety reminder: never share financial information with other users.
- Relationship labels and discovery terms, including: twink (gay/bi/trans), otter (gay/bi/trans), lipstick (lesbian/bi/trans), bear (gay/bi/trans), and butch (lesbian/bi/trans).
- Couple linking so two profiles can declare themselves as a couple.
- Unicorn profile option for a single person seeking a couple.

### Safety, moderation, and reporting
- Users can report profiles for: underage, unsafe interaction, impersonation, duplicate profile, or bigotry of any kind.
- Underage reporting: 5 unique accounts reporting within a month triggers an automatic ban until appeal with a valid ID proving legal age.
- Safety/impersonation/duplicate/bigotry: 5 reports within a month triggers a 1-year automatic ban.
- Banned users see: “Sorry, the vibe just wasn’t right.” and are told when their ban ends.
- Bans issue a permanent token visible to admins.
- Accruing 3 ban tokens blocks new or existing accounts tied to the device IMEI used at login.
- Deleted accounts must wait 3 days before creating a new account.
- Bans and ban tokens follow new accounts based on IMEI.
- Admin tools support moderating reported users and reviewing appeal documentation.
- Reporting UI includes a feedback area tied to the user account (not anonymous).

### Pricing
- **$5.99** for lifetime access.
- No subscriptions.
- No add-ons.
